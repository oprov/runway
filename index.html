Do NOT send any of these until the demo actually works

The offer is missed-call text-back, and the whole pitch depends on having a real number people can call and instantly get the auto-text. If that number doesn't reliably work yet, you're not ready to message anyone — finish the build first, keep the Twilio demo number stable so it's ready to paste into a message any time.

Warm network first (fastest path to a testimonial)

Hey [name] — random ask, but I built a tool that automatically texts back anyone who calls a business and doesn't get an answer, so they don't just hang up and call the next place. Call [demo number] and see for yourself — takes 10 seconds. Do you or anyone you know run a business that misses calls sometimes? I'll set it up for free in exchange for an honest testimonial after.

Cold outreach to a local business

Hi [name], I'm a UNH student — I built a missed-call text-back system that automatically follows up with anyone whose call to your business goes unanswered, so you're not losing them to the next search result. Curious? Call [demo number] and don't pick up (or just hang up on it) — you'll see exactly what happens in a few seconds. No meeting needed, just try it whenever.

Follow-up if they called the demo number (or didn't respond in a few days)

Hey [name], did you get a chance to try that number? If it's useful, I can have the same thing set up on your real business line by [timeframe]. If not, no worries at all — appreciate you taking a look either way.

Reality check: AI data-labeling platforms

Real, pays weekly, no sales required — but work comes in waves and dries up without warning some weeks, and onboarding requires handing over a government ID (at least one platform has had a data breach). Run 2–3 platforms at once so a dry week on one isn't a dry week on all of them. Treat it as a cash bridge, not a plan.
